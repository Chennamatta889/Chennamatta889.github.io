What these numbers leave out

This calculator assumes each kWh avoided has the same illustrative value, that usable solar generation is about 100 units per kW per 30 days and that exports have no extra value. Actual local tariffs are slab-based, solar production is seasonal, and export credits or net-metering rules differ.

It does not account for installation cost, solar subsidy, loan interest, fixed fees or maintenance. Use our solar comparison and finance tools for those separate decisions.
