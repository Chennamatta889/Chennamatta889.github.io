Plug-in electricity usage meters

Useful for understanding the consumption of compatible individual household appliances, not for estimating your entire home's solar production.

  • Check voltage, current and power rating against the appliance.
  • Look for clear kWh accumulation, credible manufacturer information and a warranty.
  • Avoid testing hardwired or high-current equipment without a qualified electrician.

Use-case: Measure how much electricity a desktop computer or refrigerator consumes over time.

Smart plugs and timers

These can help schedule compatible appliances; they do not generate electricity.

  • Check the exact maximum current and intended appliance type.
  • Do not connect high-wattage heaters, AC units or pumps unless the product is specifically rated and installed for them.
  • Understand whether Wi‑Fi, an app or a cloud account is required.

Use-case: Reduce unnecessary standby or scheduled usage.

Solar panel cleaning accessories

Maintenance needs depend on roof access, location, weather and manufacturer guidance.

  • Follow panel maker instructions on cleaning methods and warranty conditions.
  • Avoid abrasive tools and chemical mixtures that may harm coatings.
  • Never climb or work on a hazardous roof without appropriately trained professionals.

Use-case: Compare safe professional maintenance options with product claims.
