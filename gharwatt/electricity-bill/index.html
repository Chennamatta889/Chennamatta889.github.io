How to read the estimate

We divide the portion of your bill attributed to energy by the units consumed to calculate an average energy charge per unit. Indian domestic bills can use multiple tariff slabs, fixed charges and adjustments. This average does not necessarily equal the avoided marginal tariff or the value paid for solar exports.

The sizing estimate divides monthly consumption by expected usable solar generation. Roof geometry, shading, sanctioned load, inverter sizing, export rules and your DISCOM approval all affect the correct installation size.

What should I do next?

Use the system size comparison and enter the actual tariff from your bill for a more considered savings illustration. Before committing, compare vendor quotes and check requirements on the official government portal.

For current tariffs and eligibility, refer to TGERC, APERC and the official PM Surya Ghar portal. The result is an illustration, not a quotation or guaranteed bill reduction.
