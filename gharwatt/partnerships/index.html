Solar Installer Partnerships and Disclosures

We want homeowners to know exactly whether a listing or recommendation has a financial relationship behind it.

Current status: GharWatt has no active or verified installer referral agreements, paid rankings or user-lead collection form. A user-supplied smart energy meter retailer link is displayed as a potential affiliate link in the product guide and homepage. Referral earnings, if any, depend on the retailer or affiliate program; we have not independently verified the destination.

How we would work with installers

Before including a paid installer listing, we would check company registration and service geography, obtain written agreement on lead acceptance, disclose sponsorship, and publish transparent selection criteria. We would never rank installers higher solely because of a payment.

What happens to homeowner contact details?

We currently do not collect or forward name, phone number, address, electricity consumer number or bill images. Any future referral process must be opt-in, explain who receives the information, and provide a privacy notice and appropriate consent controls before activation.

Propose a public partnership discussion

For now, general feedback and non-sensitive business proposals can be submitted as a public GitHub issue. You need a GitHub account, and anything posted there is public. Do not share customer details, confidential terms, financial identifiers or private contracts. A dedicated private business contact is not configured yet.
