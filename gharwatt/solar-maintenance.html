Rooftop Solar Maintenance and Warranty Checklist

A homeowner-friendly checklist covering cleaning, inspections, warranty claims and inverter maintenance for solar systems.

A rooftop solar installation is not maintenance-free. While the frequency and procedures depend on equipment, weather and access, a good handover should make ownership predictable.

Documents to receive at handover

  • Itemized equipment list with serial numbers and warranty documents.
  • Electrical layout, isolation instructions and safe shutdown procedure.
  • Commissioning paperwork and relevant DISCOM documents.
  • Monitoring app access and an explanation of expected seasonal variation.
  • A written installer service contact and escalation route.

Routine care

Follow the manufacturer's cleaning and inspection recommendations. Dust and debris can reduce output, but climbing onto roofs or handling live electrical components can be dangerous. Use appropriately trained personnel for inaccessible or electrical work. Never assume every panel type can be cleaned with any chemical or high-pressure equipment.

Know the warranty types

Panel product warranty, panel output/performance warranty, inverter warranty and installer workmanship warranty may cover different things. Ask for exact written terms and exclusions rather than only the largest warranty number mentioned in a sales pitch.

What to do if output drops

Compare production to previous similar weather periods, verify the monitoring app is working, and contact the installer's support team. Do not open electrical equipment to diagnose inverter faults yourself.

Budget for ownership

For planning only, the GharWatt calculator assumes annual maintenance equal to 1% of gross installed cost. Actual cleaning, repairs and component replacement costs can be higher or lower. Request a written service schedule and understand what is included.
