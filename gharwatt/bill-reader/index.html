Step 1 · Choose a bill photo or PDF (optional)

What works best?

Use an English-language JPG, PNG or WebP photo under 9 MB, or a text-based PDF under 12 MB. For faded or tabular bills, select a small area of the photo before reading. You can scan the amounts and meter readings separately. Scanned PDFs without selectable text may not work: use a photo or type the details.

    Where do the units appear?
    Look for Units Consumed, Billed Units or Consumption (kWh). The meter reading number is not the same as units consumed. Charges may include slabs, fixed charges, prior dues or rebates.
