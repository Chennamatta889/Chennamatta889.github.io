PM Surya Ghar Subsidy Guide (2026)

Understand standard and special category residential central rooftop solar subsidy rules, conditions and the official application portal.

The PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana supports eligible residential rooftop solar projects through central financial assistance (CFA). This independent guide explains the published calculation structure, not a guarantee of approval or payment.

Central CFA by capacity

Residential installed capacityStandard-category states/UTsSpecial-category states/UTs
1 kW₹30,000₹33,000
2 kW₹60,000₹66,000
2.5 kW₹69,000₹75,900
3 kW or above₹78,000 cap₹85,800 cap

Under the published guidelines, the first 2 kW receive ₹30,000 per kW in standard-category states (₹33,000 in special-category locations); the next 1 kW receives ₹18,000 per kW (₹19,800 special). There is no further central CFA for additional capacity above 3 kW. The calculator applies these published rates, including fractional kW amounts, to illustrate a potential subsidy.

Which locations have the special rate?

The published guidance lists Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, northeastern states including Sikkim, Andaman & Nicobar Islands, and Lakshadweep as special-category locations. Confirm your exact current category and scheme status before proceeding.

Eligibility and important conditions

  • Central support is intended for eligible residential, grid-connected rooftop solar consumers.
  • The applicant must follow the national portal, DISCOM and applicable technical-approval processes.
  • Additional technical criteria, vendor requirements and domestic-content provisions may apply.
  • Any local or state benefits should be checked independently, not assumed to stack with the central CFA.
  • Do not deduct a presumed subsidy from a supplier invoice unless its treatment is clear and independently verified.

Where to apply

  1. Visit the government PM Surya Ghar National Portal.
  2. Register and follow the current application and DISCOM approval workflow.
  3. Review vendor eligibility and written installation terms through the official process.
  4. Keep commissioning, inspection, billing and bank documents as requested on the portal.
  5. Track the subsidy process through official channels.

Important: GharWatt India is not the government portal and cannot submit applications or confirm eligibility.
