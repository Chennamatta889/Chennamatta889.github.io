Our Calculator Methodology and Sources

Transparent methodology behind GharWatt India solar cost, subsidy, generation, savings and payback calculations.

Every estimate on this website is a calculation based on inputs and assumptions. The numbers do not represent a guaranteed installation price, an approved government benefit, an investment return or a precise electricity bill forecast.

1. Installation price

Gross cost = system capacity (kW) × installed price per kW. The illustrative default ₹60,000/kW is informed by the Delhi Solar Portal's published estimate for up to 5 kW, and is not presented as the current price in every state. Users can edit it.

2. Potential central financial assistance

The residential CFA illustration follows the MNRE residential guidelines: for standard-category locations, ₹30,000/kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000/kW for the next 1 kW, with no additional central CFA beyond 3 kW. Special-category locations use ₹33,000 and ₹19,800 respectively. These are estimates for potentially eligible households, not automatic entitlements.

3. Electricity generation

Estimated monthly production = capacity × assumed units/kW/day × 30. The 4 units/kW/day default is a modeling assumption, not a location-specific measured yield. Shading, roof orientation, weather and equipment alter output.

4. Retail-valued electricity offset

Estimated monthly energy offset = min(monthly consumed units, estimated generation × proportion valued at retail rate). The default proportion is 85%, an editable sensitivity assumption rather than a statement about official net-metering rules. Monthly gross energy-cost saving = offset units × editable effective variable electricity tariff. The calculation ignores fixed charges, slabs, time-of-use pricing, export pricing, batteries and other fees.

5. Simple payback

Gross annual energy saving = 12 × monthly gross saving. Illustrative annual maintenance = 1% × gross installed price. Net annual saving = max(0, gross annual saving − annual maintenance). Net upfront estimate = max(0, gross installed price − illustrative central CFA). Simple payback = net upfront ÷ net annual saving, if positive.

6. Quote comparison

Quoted ₹/kW = total quoted price ÷ quoted DC capacity. This tool does not perform contractor verification, price-quality ranking, equipment certification, or guarantee that quotes have identical scope. No information is transmitted to a server by this version of the tool.

Limitations and review

Guidelines can change and local tariff rules vary. Figures and interpretations should be checked against the official sources linked above and the national portal before purchasing.
