How should I read a BEE star label?

The Indian Bureau of Energy Efficiency provides official information on labelled electrical appliances. Find models of the same type, suitable capacity and labelling period; look for their specific annual electricity consumption, where available. A higher star rating on a comparable model can indicate better energy efficiency, but it does not guarantee savings for every home.
