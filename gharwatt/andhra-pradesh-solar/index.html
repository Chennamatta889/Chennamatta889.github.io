Rooftop Solar in Andhra Pradesh: Bills, Tariffs and Subsidy

A practical guide to estimating rooftop solar savings in Andhra Pradesh without guessing one tariff for every household.

Start with your own bill. Domestic energy charges can change by usage slab, consumer category and additional bill items. We do not auto-fill a single “official” rate for Andhra Pradesh. Enter the actual relevant numbers in our free calculator.

Where to verify your electricity tariff

Read the latest official retail tariff order or schedule published by Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (APERC). For your connection, also consult your electricity distribution company (APSPDCL, APCPDCL and APEPDCL) for the applicable classification, billing and net-metering conditions. The FY 2026–27 regulatory order is relevant to 2026 bills but should be rechecked when new orders take effect.

Calculate a starting solar size

Find your monthly units (kWh), total billed charges, and any fixed or other non-energy charges. The bill analyzer estimates a rough starting solar size. The system comparison lets you test 1, 2, 3 and 5 kW options using realistic inputs from your home.

Subsidy information

The central PM Surya Ghar scheme can provide eligible residential grid-connected rooftop solar consumers with central financial assistance. Under the published standard-category schedule, 1 kW corresponds to ₹30,000, 2 kW to ₹60,000 and 3 kW or above to a ₹78,000 cap. These are scheme illustrations, not promised approvals, and not every installation qualifies. Confirm current eligibility, approved vendors and application status through the official National Portal.

Before signing with a solar installer

Request detailed equipment specifications, site survey, structural plan, GST treatment, net-metering scope, technical documentation, warranties, and a payment milestone schedule. Compare your quotations using the GharWatt checklist.
