Why is this an estimate, not an exact answer?

Your electricity bill can include different pricing slabs, fixed charges, taxes, arrears or government benefits. For an illustration only, the tool assumes ₹5–₹10 per energy unit and up to ₹150/month of non-energy charges (never more than 20% of the amount). These are not official state tariffs. Actual figures may lie outside this range.

A unit on your bill means 1 kWh of electricity used. Watts or kilowatts describe power, not your monthly usage. For solar sizing, the illustration uses 4 kWh per day per kW of panels and assumes 85% of generation can offset or be credited to household use. Roof shading, export rules and eligible subsidies can change the result.

When you get your electricity bill, look for “Units Consumed”, “Consumption” or “kWh”. Check your actual tariff from Telangana's regulator or Andhra Pradesh's regulator, where applicable. Never buy a system based only on this estimate.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you decide.

Quick answers to common rooftop-solar planning questions.

Is this a final installation quotation?

No. The inputs are editable illustrations and not a vendor quotation. Real site surveys, installation scope, approvals and equipment choices can materially change the price.

Does every home receive ₹78,000?

No. The central subsidy depends on eligible residential capacity and location category, and is subject to current rules, approvals and scheme processing. At least 3 kW is needed to reach the standard-category cap, but installation alone does not guarantee payment.

Can I apply for the subsidy here?

No. Our website is independent of the Government of India. Apply through the official PM Surya Ghar National Portal and follow your DISCOM process.

Will solar eliminate my entire electricity bill?

Not necessarily. Fixed charges, minimum charges, time of use, monthly demand, exports and the state's applicable tariff rules can remain relevant even if production is high.

Is my calculator data shared with installers?

No. In this version, all calculations happen locally in your browser. We don't have an active enquiry submission or installer lead service.

Do you earn commissions from the quotes?

No installer referral partnerships, paid placements or third-party advertisements are active in this release. Any future sponsored or paid relationships should be clearly labeled.
