About GharWatt India

Learn about GharWatt India, an independent information and calculator project focused on Indian home energy decisions.

GharWatt India is an independent, early-stage website project created to make rooftop solar cost decisions easier to understand. It combines editable calculations with plain-language guides and a private contractor-quote comparison worksheet.

Why this exists

Solar quotations often include different assumptions about capacity, equipment, installation scope and after-sales support. An attractive headline saving or subsidy figure can conceal those differences. We want homeowners to see the arithmetic, check the caveats and ask better questions.

Our independence

We are not the Government of India, a DISCOM, a solar installer or a subsidy application agency. We do not approve subsidies or certify contractors. We have no active installer referral partnerships, paid advertising placements or affiliate agreements in this first release.

Our principles

  • Sources and assumptions should be visible.
  • Useful tools should be accessible without registration.
  • Customer information should not be collected before a secure purpose-specific service is ready.
  • Sponsored content, advertisements and commercial relationships must be labeled if introduced.
  • Errors should be corrected when verified.
