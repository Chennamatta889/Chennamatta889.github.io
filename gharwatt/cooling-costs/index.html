Which should I choose?

Fans circulate air but cannot lower a room's temperature like an AC. Evaporative coolers can work better in dry conditions and may be uncomfortable in humid climates. An AC controls temperature but typically uses more electricity. For ACs, start with a comfortable set point, good seals and clean filters.
