3 kW Rooftop Solar Cost in India: Worked Example

Explore a transparent hypothetical 3 kW rooftop solar system cost and subsidy example in India, with clear assumptions and caveats.

A 3 kW rooftop solar system is a common starting point for households exploring residential solar. Its actual installed cost depends on site conditions, equipment, city, taxes and vendor scope. Instead of claiming one universal market price, here is a worked example.

Illustrative example, not a market quotation

AssumptionIllustrative value
System size3 kW
Assumed installed rate₹60,000 per kW
Gross project estimate₹1,80,000
Potential standard-category central CFA, if eligible₹78,000
Illustrative net investment after CFA₹1,02,000

The ₹60,000/kW planning default is informed by the Delhi Solar Portal calculator, which gives an approximate installation cost for systems up to 5 kW. It is a reference assumption, not an India-wide offer. A real Telangana, Maharashtra or Kerala quotation may be different.

What about savings?

If the system produces 4 units per kW per day, it generates roughly 360 units in a 30-day month. The economic value depends on how much output offsets electricity charged at retail tariffs and how exports are credited. With 300 units monthly household use, an assumed effective ₹8/unit, and an 85% generation-value assumption, the simplified savings calculation offsets up to 300 units, worth about ₹2,400/month before maintenance. Fixed charges and other bill details are not included.

Before signing a contract

  • Confirm actual roof shading and structural suitability.
  • Verify the installer’s bill of materials and complete installation scope.
  • Check the net metering/DISCOM requirements for your location.
  • Check published subsidy eligibility and the timing of any payment.
  • Request warranty and maintenance terms in writing.
