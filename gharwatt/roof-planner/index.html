What could reduce usable space?

  • Water tanks, parapet shadows, trees and nearby buildings.
  • Safe walking access, emergency clearances, maintenance routes and panel tilt.
  • The roof's structural strength and waterproofing condition.
  • Panel size, orientation, wind exposure and local electrical approvals.

Our 9–12 m² per kW planning rule is an editable assumption for rough screening, not a structural assessment or guaranteed panel layout. A qualified solar installer and structural professional must confirm feasibility.
