Privacy Policy

Understand how the initial GharWatt India website handles calculator values and what may change when analytics or ads are added.

November 2026 update: The electricity-bill and solar comparison tools calculate within your browser, and the public GitHub feedback link is optional. Feedback posted to GitHub Issues is publicly visible and governed by GitHub's own privacy terms. GharWatt has not enabled private installer referrals, Google Analytics or AdSense on these pages. One user-provided retailer short link may contain affiliate attribution and may redirect via a third party. Standard GitHub Pages hosting infrastructure may still process technical access logs.

Electricity-saving checklist: On the optional seven-day challenge page, completed checkboxes are stored only in this browser using local storage. No account is needed and the website does not transmit the checklist to its server. Use “Reset checklist” to erase the stored progress, or clear the site data in your browser.

New energy tools (November 2026): The optional electricity bill photo reader uses client-side text recognition. Selecting Read Photo downloads Tesseract.js, OCR worker files and English language data from third-party content delivery servers (which may receive technical request information). The photo and recognized bill text are processed within the browser and are not uploaded to GharWatt's server or stored in its database. Close the page or select Clear to remove entered content from the current form. Always manually verify extracted results. The monthly bill tracker saves amounts and optional unit readings in local browser storage only after the visitor explicitly accepts local saving. Exported CSV files are saved to the visitor's device; the Delete action clears the saved tracker. The Share feature only opens a device sharing application or copies a summary when pressed. Optional WhatsApp buttons open WhatsApp with a generated comparison message only after the visitor clicks. CSV smart-meter exports are read locally in the browser and not stored or uploaded by GharWatt.

Effective: November 22, 2026. This policy describes our public informational site and is reviewed as functionality changes. Further advertising, affiliate tracking or referral collection will require updated disclosures and any applicable consent controls before activation.

What the calculator handles

Calculator values and quotation details are processed by JavaScript running in your browser. The site does not require accounts or transmit quote values to installers. The comparison download is generated locally in your browser. The optional AI assistant is an exception and is described below: only consented questions and optional selected bill details are sent to its backend and Gemini.

Optional GharWatt AI assistant

The GharWatt AI assistant communicates with a Supabase Edge Function and Google's Gemini service only after you tick the AI consent checkbox and click Send. Your question and any optional bill amount, units, period, dues and home/roof details you choose to share are sent to the AI provider to generate a reply. Previous short chat messages may be included in follow-up requests. Do not include your name, address, electricity consumer number, phone number, or sensitive documents in AI chat. GharWatt does not save the conversation in its database; external hosting and AI providers may process and retain technical request data under their own policies.

To provide separate anonymous usage limits without requiring sign-in, your browser creates a random, non-personal browser identifier on your first consented AI request and stores it in local or session storage. Only a daily secret-salted hash of that ID is recorded in the backend quota table, alongside the UTC date and count. GharWatt does not use the IP address as the visitor's five-message quota identity. An individual browser may send up to five AI requests per UTC day; a 400-request daily limit and 10-request rolling 60-second limit are shared by the whole site. Rejected minute-limited attempts do not count against daily allowances. Short-lived request timestamps are stored without the browser ID or message. Visitors on the same network have separate browser allowances unless the overall website limit has been reached. You may delete your locally stored ID by clearing site data, but this can reset the browser quota and does not remove provider records. AI responses are not official billing advice.

Analytics, advertisements and cookies

The current site does not include Google Analytics, AdSense or other third-party analytics/advertising scripts. Product spotlight cards link to independent buying advice. If a card contains an explicitly labeled affiliate link, opening it takes you to a third-party shop or affiliate redirect where its operator may use cookies or attribution technology. Our site does not collect or store affiliate clicks itself. We only display paid links after they are configured, and any displayed link is clearly marked. Hosting providers may process necessary HTTP request and security logs under their own policies after deployment. If advertising, analytics or lead forms are enabled later, this policy and any legally required consent controls must be updated before activation.

External links

Links to official government websites, documentation and other external resources operate under those websites' own policies. Opening one may transfer request information to that site.

Security and retention

The core electricity calculators do not retain your values in a server database. The optional AI assistant does use a server-side daily quota table containing pseudonymous daily visitor hashes and counts, but does not save questions or bill data. The monthly tracker stores only the local data you explicitly choose to save. A future installer enquiry service would require purpose-specific permission, secure processing, a clear retention policy, and an accessible privacy/contact channel.

Updates

We will update this page if the site introduces advertising, analytics, active submissions or other personal-data processing. This public release currently has no paid advertisements or active installer enquiry service.
