Compare Rooftop Solar Installer Quotations

Understand what to check in multiple rooftop solar quotations: capacity, panels, inverter, installation scope, warranties and more.

Two quotes may show the same kilowatt capacity but deliver different equipment, support and long-term reliability. Comparing only the total price can conceal important differences.

Start with a like-for-like comparison

Write down the DC module capacity (kWp), inverter AC capacity, brand and specific models, mounting structure, panel technology, and whether quoted prices include GST and installation. A 3 kW package with a different inverter or roof structure is not necessarily identical.

Quote comparison checklist

  • Panel and inverter details: Manufacturer, model, production warranty, performance warranty and inverter warranty terms.
  • Electrical safety: Earthing, lightning protection, isolators, cables, conduits and surge protection.
  • Roof works: Structure material, corrosion protection, waterproofing and roof reinforcement if needed.
  • Approvals: DISCOM documentation, net meter, technical inspection and fees, and who manages each step.
  • Commercial terms: GST, transport, installation, milestones, cancellation and warranty claims.
  • After-sales: Who responds to faults, monitoring access, cleaning guidance and service visits.

Normalize the pricing

Calculate quoted amount ÷ installed kW to compare the headline cost per kW, then adjust for differences in equipment and included work. A cheaper price per kW is not proof of better value. The downloadable worksheet on this website can help structure the comparison.

Questions worth asking

  1. Can you provide a complete bill of materials with model numbers?
  2. Does this include all roof, electrical and approval-related work?
  3. Are warranty claims handled by the installer or manufacturer, and are travel/service costs included?
  4. Who is responsible for timeline delays, rectification and damage?
  5. How will you handle generation monitoring and handover documentation?
