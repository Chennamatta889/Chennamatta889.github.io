How to Choose Rooftop Solar System Size

Understand kW sizing for Indian rooftops using electricity consumption, roof conditions and generation assumptions.

The best solar system size depends on what your household consumes, when it consumes energy, available shadow-free roof area, and how local grid rules value exports. Bigger is not automatically more economical.

Start with real electricity consumption

Review 12 months of electricity bills if possible. A single summer or winter bill can misrepresent your annual use. Separate variable energy charges from fixed fees and understand whether time-of-day pricing applies.

Estimate daily generation

A simple planning estimate is monthly generation = kW capacity × assumed units per kW per day × 30. At 4 units/kW/day, a 3 kW installation generates about 360 units monthly in this simplified model. Actual production varies with location, panel orientation, shading, temperature and equipment losses.

Check the physical roof

Have a competent installer assess structural suitability, available unobstructed area, tilt, wind loads, waterproofing and any future construction. Do not rely solely on a satellite photograph or a generic square-foot rule.

Account for local tariff and net metering

Some households may export excess energy during daylight hours and import during evenings. Local DISCOM accounting and compensation rules affect financial outcomes. Our planner uses an editable proportion of generation valued at the retail rate, so you can test conservative scenarios without assuming all exports earn full retail credit.

Request multiple design proposals

Ask installers to present expected monthly production, equipment details, monitoring capabilities and their assumptions. Compare their estimates with realistic household demand before selecting a size.
