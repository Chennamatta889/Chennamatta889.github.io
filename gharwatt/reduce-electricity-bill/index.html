How to Reduce Your Electricity Bill — Simple Steps

You do not need to understand watts, tariffs or solar panels to start saving electricity. Try these home habits first, then check if your next bill uses fewer units.

Start here: The electricity you avoid using is usually the simplest saving. Before buying products or installing solar panels, try reducing waste safely. This guide is free and has no guaranteed percentage savings.

First, understand two words on your bill

Bill amount₹

The total money you pay. It can also include fixed charges, taxes, earlier dues or credits.

Units consumedkWh

The quantity of electricity you used. One unit means 1 kilowatt-hour (kWh).

For example, a 10-watt LED light running five hours a day for 30 days uses about 1.5 units. But you do not need to calculate this by hand. If you know only your bill amount, use our bill-only calculator for an approximate starting point.

9 simple ways to use less electricity

Begin with the free changes. You do not need to buy smart plugs, new fans or solar panels before trying these steps.

01No purchase needed

Turn off things nobody is using

Switch off room lights, fans and televisions when you leave. Keep essential equipment such as medical devices, safety lighting and refrigerators running as needed.

02Low-cost change

Use LED bulbs where suitable

If you still use an old incandescent bulb, an appropriate LED can provide similar light using less electricity. Check brightness and fitting before changing it.

03Cooling

Use fans and AC thoughtfully

Try a fan before AC if comfortable. If using AC, start around 24–26°C when suitable, keep doors/windows closed and clean filters following the maker's instructions.

04Water heating

Heat only the water you need

Avoid leaving an electric geyser running unnecessarily. Switch it off according to the manufacturer's instructions; never modify electrical wiring yourself.

05Kitchen

Use the fridge efficiently

Keep the door closed, check worn door seals, follow the maker's ventilation guidance and keep the correct food-safe temperature. Do not switch off a fridge storing perishables.

06Water pump

Check your water pump and leaks

Avoid running a pump longer than needed. Fix dripping taps and ask a qualified technician to inspect faulty pumps or automatic switches.

07Laundry

Use washing machines sensibly

Where the manufacturer permits, wash full but not overloaded loads, select suitable lower-temperature programmes, and use sunlight to dry clothes when practical.

08Buying decision

Compare BEE stars before replacing appliances

When an appliance is worn out, compare the Indian BEE star label and labelled electricity consumption for similar-sized models. Do not replace working equipment just for a star rating without checking cost.

09Monthly habit

Check your bill each month

Compare billed units (kWh), number of billing days and extra charges, not just rupees. If usage jumps without explanation, contact your DISCOM for a legitimate meter/bill check; never tamper with the meter.

A simple example of why LEDs help

Suppose a 60-watt incandescent bulb is replaced by a suitable 9-watt LED, and it runs five hours each day. The difference is roughly 7.65 units in 30 days. At a purely illustrative ₹8 per unit, that is about ₹61. Actual savings depend on the original bulb, the replacement, hours used and the applicable tariff.

You may save much less if you already use efficient lighting.

What if my bill is still high?

Compare the actual units over two full billing periods, check if there were more billed days, and look for old dues or adjustments. Hot weather, visitors, more AC use or new appliances can increase usage. If the bill or meter reading appears wrong, contact your electricity distribution company for an official check. Do not open a meter, bypass electrical devices or attempt wiring repairs.
