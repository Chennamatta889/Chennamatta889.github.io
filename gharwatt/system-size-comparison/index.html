Why might a larger solar system not save proportionally more?

The calculator caps estimated credited solar units at your stated monthly usage. A system producing more than you consume may have a different value depending on local net-metering or billing policy. This model deliberately assigns no extra savings to surplus generation.

What does payback include?

Simple payback is estimated net installed cost divided by annual energy savings less an assumed annual maintenance provision of 1% of gross installed cost. It excludes interest, degradation, inflation, financing, insurance, export credits and future replacement costs. It should not be treated as a financial guarantee.
