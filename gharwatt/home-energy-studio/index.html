Installer selection checklist

  • Check registered-vendor eligibility directly.
  • Compare DC panel size vs inverter AC rating.
  • Confirm GST, structure, earthing and metering charges.
  • Demand written workmanship and equipment warranties.
  • Never send advance payments without verification.

GharWatt does not currently operate an installer lead marketplace or certify contractors.
