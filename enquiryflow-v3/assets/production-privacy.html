Privacy information for student enquiries

Updated 22 November 2026. The responsible institute must approve its identity and contact before real intake is enabled.

Checking institute details… If verification fails, do not submit personal information.

Information collected

When you choose to submit an enquiry, we collect your name, verified Google email address, requested course or batch (if selected), your question, your consent, and a submission timestamp. Your Google account identifier is recorded solely to enforce a per-account submission cooldown. We do not request payment card details or identity documents; please do not include sensitive information in your question.

Why we use it

Authorized institute staff use the enquiry to answer your admissions question and manage follow-up. The data is not publicly displayed on the student site and is not used for advertising. Email notifications are not automatically sent by this website.

Access, hosting and security

Enquiries are stored in the institute’s Firebase Cloud Firestore project. Authorized staff accounts can view enquiries under Firebase security rules; public visitors cannot list or read them. Google Firebase and authentication services process data as their service providers. No web analytics are used on this staging page.

Retention and deletion

Enquiries are retained until the institute owner deletes them from the staff portal. Automatic deletion is not configured. Contact the institute to ask about access, corrections, deletion, or its retention practices before submitting.
