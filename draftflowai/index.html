Good to know

Questions?
We've got answers.

Who is DraftflowAI for?

IT professionals looking for a more organized way to find relevant career opportunities, from software development to infrastructure, data, testing and operations.

Do you guarantee a job or interview?

No. DraftflowAI supports your job search through outreach, tracking and eligible profile services. Hiring decisions are made by employers.

How do I get started?

Contact us to discuss your target roles, resume and plan requirements. This website is informational; no payment or resume upload is processed here.

How will I know what has been done?

Each plan includes an application tracking sheet to keep outreach organized and help reduce duplicate contacts.

Are the email numbers guaranteed?

No. The numbers are monthly maximums. Relevant opportunities, contact availability and applicable outreach limits can affect actual activity.

What are the cancellation and refund terms?

Ask for the applicable subscription, cancellation and refund terms in writing before making any payment or commitment. This website does not process payments.

How should I share my resume?

Discuss an appropriate sharing method with us first. Do not enter passwords, identity documents or payment details on this website.
